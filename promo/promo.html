How the two-way sync with Apple Reminders works
It never talks to iCloud directly.
It only writes to Reminders on the Mac (EventKit). iCloud carries it the rest of the way.
Inside your Mac
Handled by Apple
EKEventStore · EKReminder · a "TodoBar" list created in your iCloud account
When does it sync?
Either side can trigger it, and bursts are merged into one run.
TRIGGER 1 · MAC
todos.json changes
Saved from the panel or the todo CLI
store.onChange → sync.request()
TRIGGER 2 · IPHONE
Reminders changes
An edit made on the phone arrives via iCloud
and fires EKEventStoreChanged
TIMELINE
0.6 s debounce · every new event resets the timer
run() — one sync
4 events → 1 run
How does it know who changed?
sync.json remembers how things looked at the last sync. Compare all three and the changed side shows.
